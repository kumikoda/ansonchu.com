Auto-research

2026 · project · writeup in progress

A small autonomous-research project over Gym environments: an agent loop that proposes an experiment, runs it, looks at the result, and decides what to try next — then writes up what it found.

The interesting part is not the RL itself, which is deliberately simple. It's the loop around it: what makes an experiment worth running, how the agent should read its own results, and where the loop goes wrong without a human in it.
